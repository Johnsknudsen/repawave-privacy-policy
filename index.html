Privacy Policy for RepaWave

Last updated: April 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the RepaWave app handles information.

Information We Collect

RepaWave may use Bluetooth and location-related permissions on Android in order to scan for and connect to nearby Bluetooth devices.

The app may display device-related information such as battery data, telemetry, settings, and connection status from compatible hardware.

We do not sell personal data.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the app’s core functionality, including finding devices, connecting to them, showing device data, and allowing the user to control supported hardware.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except where required by law or necessary to operate services explicitly used by the app.

Data Storage

Some settings or device information may be stored locally on your device to improve app functionality.

Your Choices

You can stop Bluetooth scanning or remove the app at any time. You can also manage app permissions in your device settings.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact: johnsknudsen88@gmail.com
